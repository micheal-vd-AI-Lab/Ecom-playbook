FAQ

Before you start.

Do I need a big budget?

No. You can learn and validate with a small budget. Never spend money you cannot afford to lose.

Is this only for dropshipping?

No. The same research, offer, acquisition, conversion and unit-economic principles apply to many e-commerce models.

Which platform should I use?

Choose a platform that fits your skills, budget and business model. The course principles are platform-agnostic.

How do I know when to scale?

Scale when your economics are understood, tracking is reliable and you can explain why customers are buying — not simply because one day performed well.
